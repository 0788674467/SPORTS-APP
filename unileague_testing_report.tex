\section{User Acceptance Testing (UAT)}
UAT was conducted with real stakeholders at Mountains of the Moon University to validate whether the system solved their practical challenges. Participants included 2 Sports Coordinators, 4 Coaches, 2 Referees, and 10 Spectators.

\subsection{System Usability Scale (SUS) Evaluation}
Following the UAT sessions, participants completed a System Usability Scale (SUS) questionnaire to evaluate the ease of use of UniLeague. 

\begin{itemize}[label=\checkmark]
    \item \textbf{Administrators \& Coordinators:} Reported a dramatic reduction in paperwork and improved tracking of team payments and fixtures. \textbf{Average SUS Score: 85.4} (Excellent).
    \item \textbf{Coaches:} Found the drag-and-drop Lineup Builder and substitution mechanics highly intuitive. \textbf{Average SUS Score: 82.0} (Good/Excellent).
    \item \textbf{Referees:} Appreciated the digital match card, reducing post-match reporting delays. \textbf{Average SUS Score: 80.5} (Good).
    \item \textbf{Spectators:} Highly valued the real-time score updates and 3D pitch visualizations. \textbf{Average SUS Score: 88.0} (Excellent).
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Overall System Usability Score: 83.9} — This indicates a high level of system acceptability and readiness for deployment within the MMU sports ecosystem.

\section{Conclusion}
The testing results indicate that UniLeague functions correctly across all major modules. The integration between the Flutter frontend, Supabase database, and real-time Socket services is robust. User acceptance testing confirms that the application effectively digitalizes manual workflows, resolves fixture scheduling conflicts, and provides real-time engagement for spectators, fully satisfying the objectives set out for this project.
